## Copy and design choices
Audience: engineering leaders, AI teams and growing businesses assessing a cloud provider.
Lead with application outcomes and explain product use cases. Avoid unverified uptime, price superiority, compliance and scale claims. Use concrete decisions and plain language. Keep technical sources linked in the article.
Visual system: white and cool neutral surfaces, cobalt accents, deep navy architecture and estimate panels, generous spacing, structured typography. Use architecture geometry to explain the service relationship rather than stock server imagery.

## Scope and assumptions
Brand name was not supplied, so Stratum Cloud is replaceable. Provider inventory, contractual terms and commercial rates were not supplied. All calculator defaults are illustrative. No competitor pricing is represented as live data.
