Understand the cost model ↗

Know what you’re estimating

No hidden assumptions.

Use this as a planning model. Actual charges depend on the provider, region, instance specification and contract.

How is the estimate calculated?

GPU and VM costs equal quantity × hourly rate × compute hours. Storage costs equal GB × monthly GB rate. Database and cluster costs equal count × monthly rate. Transfer equals outbound GB × rate.

Does lower usage reduce every charge?

No. Compute hours only affect GPU and VM charges in this model. Provisioned storage and monthly service fees stay in the estimate until you reduce their quantities.

How do I include migration costs?

Migration is project-specific and excluded here. Request an assessment that covers data transfer, overlapping environments, engineering effort and any downtime constraints.
