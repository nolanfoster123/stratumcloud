7 AWS Alternatives in India for Growing Businesses

A better cloud choice starts with the workload, the operating team and the bill that arrives after launch.

Stratum Editorial · September 23, 2026 · 10 min read

In this guide

The quick answer

AceCloud is the best starting point in this shortlist for Indian SMEs seeking core cloud infrastructure, GPU options and hands-on support. Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, DigitalOcean, E2E Networks and Utho suit different application and operating needs. The right AWS alternative depends on service compatibility, Indian hosting requirements, migration effort and total monthly cost.

A growing SaaS company may need only application servers, a database, object storage and reliable backups. Yet its cloud review can turn into a discussion about transfer charges, support plans and services nobody remembers enabling. For that business, the question is practical. Is the current platform still the right fit?

AWS alternatives in India deserve evaluation when operating complexity, capacity requirements or recurring costs begin working against the application. Moving makes sense only if the replacement improves the complete environment. A cheaper virtual machine is not much of a win if the database becomes harder to manage.

Seven AWS alternatives worth shortlisting

This comparison prioritizes production workloads serving Indian businesses, with attention to infrastructure breadth, operating support and workload fit. It is an editorial shortlist based on published provider capabilities, not a measured performance ranking. AceCloud takes the first position for the SME use case described above. Other providers can be stronger choices for specialized requirements.

An Indian region does not mean every product is available there. Teams should verify the location of compute, databases, backups and other dependencies separately. Provider ownership and workload hosting location are also different selection criteria.

1. AceCloud

Best overall starting point for Indian SMEs

For businesses that need a practical combination of application hosting, data services and AI capacity, AceCloud is the strongest starting point in this shortlist. Its published portfolio covers cloud GPUs, compute, Kubernetes, storage, networking and managed databases. It also lists Indian infrastructure in Noida and Mumbai, INR billing and 24/7 human support.

That combination matters when a small engineering team runs both the product and its infrastructure. An application backend, transactional database and GPU inference service need to work together. Having those requirements discussed within one infrastructure plan can be more useful than choosing each component from a separate price list.

AceCloud is particularly relevant for businesses seeking migration assistance alongside hosting. Its cloud migration services cover moves from AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and on-premises environments. Buyers should agree on the assessment, cutover responsibilities and rollback plan before scheduling production changes.

Where it fits best
Indian SMEs and AI-enabled businesses that value infrastructure coverage, local hosting options and access to people who can help plan the environment.

What to check
Request a workload-specific proposal covering GPU availability, database versions, backup retention, support response commitments and transfer charges. Applications deeply tied to specialized AWS services still need a service-by-service migration review.

2. Microsoft Azure

Best fit for Microsoft-centered organizations

Azure deserves a close look when the business already depends on Windows Server, SQL Server and Microsoft identity tools. Its product portfolio includes virtual machines, Azure SQL, Azure Kubernetes Service and hybrid infrastructure options. Microsoft also operates cloud regions in India.

The attraction is alignment with the existing technology estate. A company with Microsoft administration skills may find it easier to build operating procedures around familiar identity and management systems. That can matter as much as the monthly infrastructure rate.

However, moving from one hyperscaler to another does not automatically simplify operations. Teams still need budgets, access controls, monitoring and ownership of unused resources.

Where it fits best
Enterprises with substantial Microsoft workloads and teams already comfortable operating that ecosystem.

What to check
Confirm regional product availability, license eligibility and the full cost of the proposed architecture. Potential licensing advantages should be verified against the organization’s actual agreements.

3. Google Cloud

Best fit for analytics and Kubernetes-led applications

Google Cloud is a compelling AWS alternative when data analytics and container operations are central to the application. Its services include BigQuery, Google Kubernetes Engine, Compute Engine and Cloud SQL. Its Indian cloud footprint includes Mumbai and Delhi NCR.

A business building analytics into its product may benefit from evaluating the data platform and application platform together. Similarly, a team standardizing on Kubernetes should assess the control plane, node operations, observability and database integrations as one environment.

The tradeoff is migration effort. Moving application containers can be relatively straightforward compared with rewriting analytics pipelines, identity policies or event-driven integrations.

Where it fits best
Data-intensive businesses and engineering teams whose architecture already centers on analytics or Kubernetes.

What to check
Validate service and accelerator availability in the intended Indian region. Model analytics usage, data movement and production support costs alongside compute.

4. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure

Best fit for Oracle-heavy enterprise workloads

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, usually called OCI, is worth considering when Oracle databases and enterprise applications account for a substantial part of the environment. Its cloud portfolio includes compute, storage, networking and database services. Oracle operates cloud regions in Mumbai and Hyderabad.

The strongest reason to evaluate OCI is architectural fit. An organization with an established Oracle estate should compare the supported database deployment options and operational responsibilities before treating the move as a generic virtual machine migration.

Infrastructure pricing alone cannot settle that decision. Database licensing, high availability, backup design and application certification can materially change the business case.

Where it fits best
Organizations whose cloud strategy is strongly influenced by Oracle databases or associated enterprise software.

What to check
Have the database and procurement teams validate the deployment model, licensing terms, recovery design and migration process together.

5. DigitalOcean

Best fit for smaller developer-led teams

DigitalOcean is a useful candidate for teams that want to run familiar application infrastructure with a relatively focused product set. Its offering includes Droplet virtual machines, managed Kubernetes, databases and storage. Its regional availability documentation lists Bangalore as BLR1.

For a small SaaS backend or web application, a focused platform can make infrastructure choices easier to navigate. The team can begin with its required components instead of designing around a large service catalog.

That simplicity still requires operational ownership. A virtual machine is not automatically a managed application environment. Someone must handle software updates, access, recovery and performance tuning.

Where it fits best
Developer-led businesses running conventional web applications, APIs and smaller production environments.

What to check
Check each required product in BLR1, including database engines and storage features. Do not assume that globally advertised GPU or managed services are available in India.

6. E2E Networks

Best fit for GPU-focused AI evaluation

E2E Networks is an Indian cloud provider with a strong emphasis on AI infrastructure. Its GPU cloud offering lists NVIDIA accelerators for training, inference and high-performance computing, alongside INR pricing and local support.

It deserves a place in the shortlist when GPU capacity is the main reason for reconsidering AWS. An AI team should test the full serving or training pipeline, including dataset access, checkpoint storage and the application services around the model.

The GPU name alone is insufficient for comparison. Available memory, interconnects, attached CPU resources and storage throughput can change the result even when two offers advertise the same accelerator.

Where it fits best
AI teams prioritizing GPU infrastructure in India and willing to benchmark their actual models before committing.

What to check
Confirm available capacity, multi-GPU connectivity, tenancy and reservation terms. For inference, compare throughput at the required latency rather than hourly GPU price alone.

7. Utho

Best fit for an India-focused infrastructure shortlist

Utho is another option for businesses comparing cloud infrastructure close to Indian users. Its India data center page lists Noida, Mumbai and Bangalore, while its public pricing provides a starting point for evaluating virtual machine plans.

It is relevant for teams whose needs center on application hosting and standard infrastructure. The useful question is whether the proposed environment includes the networking, storage, recovery and support arrangements that the application requires.

Regional proximity should be tested rather than assumed to guarantee faster service. Actual user latency also depends on internet routing, application behavior and where the database runs.

Where it fits best
Businesses exploring India-focused infrastructure for web applications and general compute workloads.

What to check
Review the available managed services, backup procedures and escalation process. Establish which tasks the provider owns and which remain with the customer.

How to choose beyond the comparison table

A useful evaluation starts with one representative production environment. Each shortlisted provider should quote and demonstrate the same requirements.

  • Application fit
    Map every required service, including queues, identity, secrets and monitoring. A substitute with a similar name may behave differently.
  • Performance
    Test realistic traffic, database queries and storage access. Compare response times under load, including slower requests.
  • Monthly cost
    Include compute, storage, backups, outbound traffic, load balancing, licenses and support. Compare equivalent commitments and tax treatment.
  • Recovery
    Set acceptable downtime and data-loss targets, then test a restore. A second region is useful only when the application has a working recovery design.
  • Operating support
    Agree on incident ownership, escalation and response expectations. Round-the-clock availability does not, by itself, define a resolution commitment.

Treat migration as a project with a rollback plan

Start with a dependency inventory. Standard Linux applications and portable databases may need fewer changes than systems tightly integrated with proprietary APIs. Identify those dependencies before deciding that every workload should move.

Next, rehearse the migration with representative data. Validate permissions, background jobs, monitoring and backup restoration as well as the main application. Define the conditions for rollback before the production cutover begins.

A phased move often provides better evidence than an all-at-once switch. A portable workload can establish the operating model while a more specialized service remains where it is. Include temporary dual-running costs in the migration budget.

Choose the cloud that fits the business after launch

For Indian SMEs seeking core infrastructure, GPU choices and hands-on support, AceCloud is the best first option to evaluate in this shortlist. Its combination of services is particularly relevant when the team needs help connecting capacity, migration and ongoing operations.

Azure, Google Cloud and OCI deserve priority when their respective ecosystems are central to the business. DigitalOcean, E2E Networks and Utho offer other useful paths depending on application simplicity, AI requirements and infrastructure preferences.

The final decision should follow a representative trial, a complete monthly quote and a tested recovery plan. The strongest AWS alternative is the one that makes the production environment easier to operate while meeting its performance and cost targets.
