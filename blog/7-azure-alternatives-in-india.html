7 Azure Alternatives in India for Production Workloads

A successful move starts with understanding what the application depends on, then choosing the cloud that fits.

Stratum Editorial · September 23, 2026 · 10 min read

The quick answer

AceCloud is the best starting point in this shortlist for Indian SMEs seeking core infrastructure, GPU options and hands-on support. AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, DigitalOcean, E2E Networks and Utho suit other priorities. Before leaving Azure, map application dependencies, verify regional services and compare migration effort, licensing, recovery requirements and the complete monthly operating cost.

A production application may run on only a few virtual machines, yet depend on Azure SQL, Blob Storage, Key Vault and managed identities behind the scenes. When the bill rises or a new GPU requirement appears, moving those machines can look like the obvious answer. The dependencies make the decision more interesting.

For Indian businesses evaluating Azure alternatives, a useful comparison asks what should move, what must change and what should stay. The best destination improves the operating model without creating an avoidable rewrite or a recovery gap.

When does an Azure alternative deserve a serious review?

A review makes sense when the current environment no longer matches the workload or the team. Stable resource demand may justify comparing committed monthly proposals. A small operations team may want more direct infrastructure assistance. An AI feature may require a different accelerator configuration or available capacity.

India hosting alone is not a reason to assume Azure must be replaced. Microsoft already operates Indian cloud regions. The relevant questions are whether the required services are available in the chosen region and where application data, backups and other copies are configured to reside.

Nor does using Microsoft 365 mean every application must run on Azure. Business productivity software, user identity and application hosting are related decisions, but they are not identical. Each integration needs an explicit review.

Azure may remain the right home for deeply integrated workloads. A portable API or batch-processing service can still be evaluated separately, provided the cost and complexity of connecting environments are included.

Seven Azure alternatives at a glance

This editorial shortlist prioritizes production workload fit, operating assistance and migration practicality. It is based on published capabilities rather than an independent performance benchmark. AceCloud leads for the Indian SME use case, while other providers can be a stronger match for specific platforms or dependencies.

1. AceCloud

Best starting point for Indian SMEs

AceCloud is the strongest first option in this comparison for businesses seeking core production infrastructure with human support. Its platform covers compute, GPUs, Kubernetes, storage, networking and managed databases, with Indian infrastructure in Noida and Mumbai.

For a team hosting application servers and background workers, AceCloud Cloud Compute provides a relevant starting point. The proposal should connect CPU and memory sizing with storage performance, networking and the database layer, rather than treating the virtual machine as the entire application.

This is particularly useful when a small team wants help planning the move. AceCloud’s cloud migration services include Azure as a source environment. The scope should identify assessment, data transfer, testing and cutover responsibilities.

Best suited to
Indian SMEs and AI-enabled businesses seeking a coordinated infrastructure plan and access to operating assistance.

Validate before choosing
Confirm service compatibility, Indian capacity, support response commitments and the complete quote. Azure-specific managed services require individual replacements or a plan to keep them connected.

2. Amazon Web Services

Best for a broad managed-service ecosystem

AWS is a logical alternative when the application needs extensive managed infrastructure and the engineering team can operate a large cloud environment. Its Indian footprint includes Mumbai and Hyderabad, documented on its India regions page.

The benefit is the ability to evaluate application hosting, databases, messaging and event processing within one ecosystem. That can be valuable when the business is already planning architectural changes.

However, matching product categories is not the same as matching behavior. A replacement for an Azure queue, database or event service may differ in delivery guarantees, authentication or operational limits. Those differences need application testing.

Best suited to
Teams that need a broad platform and have a clear reason to redesign around its managed services.

Validate before choosing
Include networking, monitoring, support and service integration work in the business case. Moving between hyperscalers does not automatically simplify operations.

3. Google Cloud

Best for analytics and Kubernetes-led applications

Google Cloud deserves attention when analytics, data processing or Kubernetes operations drive the decision. Its platform includes BigQuery, Google Kubernetes Engine, Compute Engine and Cloud SQL. Its location directory includes Mumbai and Delhi NCR.

A product team adding analytics may benefit from reviewing the data pipeline and application platform together. Similarly, a team operating many container services should compare the complete cluster operating model rather than just worker prices.

Portability needs to be demonstrated. Kubernetes manifests may still reference Azure-specific storage classes, load balancers, identity bindings or secrets integrations.

Best suited to
Data-intensive businesses and teams with a clear analytics or container-platform reason to move.

Validate before choosing
Test regional service availability, application integrations and the cost of repeated data movement. Avoid separating large datasets from their frequent consumers without measuring the effect.

4. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure

Best for Oracle-centered enterprise workloads

OCI is worth evaluating when Oracle databases or enterprise applications strongly influence infrastructure requirements. Its cloud portfolio covers compute, networking, storage and database services, with Indian regions in Mumbai and Hyderabad.

The decision is most persuasive when it solves an existing Oracle deployment requirement. The database team should assess supported configurations, recovery and application certification before procurement compares the rates.

A generic VM comparison can miss the largest costs in an enterprise application. Database licensing, management and availability design may outweigh the difference in compute pricing.

Best suited to
Organizations whose production architecture is shaped by Oracle software and database operations.

Validate before choosing
Review the deployment model and licensing with qualified internal owners. Test the actual application and database combination in the intended region.

5. DigitalOcean

Best for smaller teams with conventional applications

DigitalOcean is relevant when the application can run on a relatively focused combination of virtual machines, managed databases, storage and Kubernetes. Its regional availability documentation lists Bangalore as BLR1.

A portable Linux application may not need the breadth of Azure’s service catalog. A narrower environment can make day-to-day choices easier, provided it covers the required production capabilities.

The main qualification is what the application currently consumes. Replacing Azure App Service with a virtual machine, for example, can shift runtime maintenance and deployment responsibilities back to the customer.

Best suited to
Developer-led businesses running conventional APIs, web applications and background jobs.

Validate before choosing
Verify every required product in Bangalore, including database engines and storage features. Compare management responsibilities as well as resource specifications.

6. E2E Networks

Best for a GPU-focused shortlist

E2E Networks is an Indian provider with a strong emphasis on GPU infrastructure for AI and machine learning. It is relevant when accelerator capacity or configuration is the main reason to review Azure.

The test should use the team’s actual model, precision, input lengths and concurrency. Matching the GPU name is insufficient if CPU resources, storage throughput or multi-GPU connectivity differ.

Training and inference also have different priorities. Training may need sustained throughput and checkpoint performance, while interactive inference needs acceptable latency under realistic concurrency.

Best suited to
AI teams evaluating India-based GPU capacity with a representative benchmark.

Validate before choosing
Confirm available hardware, tenancy, reservation terms and supporting data services. A working GPU trial does not establish that the surrounding application is ready to migrate.

7. Utho

Best for India-focused infrastructure comparisons

Utho is another candidate for general cloud infrastructure evaluation. Its published Indian data center locations include Noida, Mumbai and Bangalore.

It is most relevant when the requirement centers on familiar application infrastructure rather than a direct replacement for Azure’s entire managed-service catalog. Start with the server, storage, networking and recovery configuration the application needs.

Location should be validated through application tests. A nearby data center does not by itself establish lower end-user latency or better database performance.

Best suited to
Businesses comparing India-focused hosting for standard application stacks.

Validate before choosing
Confirm supported configurations, backup restoration, managed-service scope and escalation arrangements. Document who owns operating system and application maintenance.

Map Azure dependencies before comparing quotes

The easiest component to move is not always the most important one. Inventory the connections between services so the destination proposal accounts for the whole request path.

Current Azure componentMigration question
Virtual machines and disksCan the operating system, boot configuration and data volumes be recreated and validated?
Azure SQL or managed databasesWhich engine features, management tasks and recovery capabilities must be preserved?
Blob StorageDoes the application need API, SDK, permission or signed-link changes?
AKSWhich storage, ingress, networking and identity settings depend on Azure?
Managed identities and Key VaultHow will workloads authenticate and retrieve secrets at the destination?
App Service or FunctionsWhat replaces the runtime, deployment process, triggers and scaling behavior?
Monitoring and recoveryHow will alerts, logs, backups and restore procedures be recreated?

Azure managed identities illustrate why this matters. Microsoft’s documentation describes how Azure resources obtain an automatically managed identity in Microsoft Entra ID. A workload moved to another cloud needs an appropriate authentication design. Its existing managed-identity behavior should not be assumed to follow the VM.

Likewise, an S3-compatible bucket is not a direct Azure Blob API replacement. A container image can be portable while the storage client inside it remains tied to Azure.

Compare complete operating costs

Use recent usage and billing data to build the Azure baseline. Include existing discounts and commitments so the comparison reflects what the business actually pays. Then ask each provider to quote an equivalent availability and support design.

  • Recurring infrastructure
    Compute, storage, databases, backups, load balancing and outbound traffic.
  • Operating services
    Monitoring, registry storage, security tooling and support.
  • Software and contracts
    Applicable licenses, existing commitments and destination terms, reviewed by the responsible owners.
  • Transition work
    Engineering changes, data transfer, temporary dual running and testing.

A useful break-even estimate divides the one-time migration cost by the expected net monthly saving. The monthly saving must include added operating work and any services left running in Azure. If that saving is uncertain, a short trial is more informative than a precise-looking payback figure.

Prove the destination before the production cutover

Begin with a representative workload rather than the easiest empty server. Test normal traffic, background jobs, authentication and data access. Compare slower-request latency as well as averages.

For databases and object storage, define how changes made during the transfer will be captured. A copied dataset can already be stale before cutover. Rehearse the final synchronization and verify integrity using methods appropriate to the data.

Restore a backup at the destination and test monitoring with a controlled failure. Agree on rollback triggers and account for writes accepted after the switch. Simply redirecting traffic to the old environment may expose outdated data.

A phased move can be sensible, but it introduces cross-cloud networking, cost and troubleshooting responsibilities. Keep the boundary deliberate and avoid placing frequently communicating components far apart without measurement.

Choose a better operating fit

AceCloud is the best first option in this shortlist for Indian SMEs seeking core infrastructure, GPU options and hands-on assistance. AWS, Google Cloud and OCI deserve attention when their specific platforms match the application. DigitalOcean, E2E Networks and Utho address other requirements.

The strongest decision starts with dependencies, not a provider ranking. Validate the proposed architecture, compare complete costs and rehearse recovery. A successful Azure alternative should improve production operations while preserving the capabilities the business relies on.
