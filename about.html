About Stratum

Good infrastructure
starts with better questions.

What are you building? What happens when demand grows? What can’t afford to go offline? These are the questions that shape our approach.

Our perspective

Make the cloud serve
the application.

A powerful server is only useful when the rest of the system can keep up.

AI services need more than GPU capacity. Databases need more than CPU cores. Production applications need an architecture that connects compute, storage, networking and operations.

Stratum brings that thinking into one place. Our focus is a clear infrastructure plan, from initial sizing to migration and ongoing capacity decisions.

What guides us

Clarity at every layer.

01

Fit before footprint

Choose infrastructure for the actual workload. Begin with memory, traffic, data access and operational requirements, then size the resources.

02

Explain the trade-offs

More redundancy costs more. Higher utilization can reduce headroom. Good decisions make those trade-offs visible before deployment.

03

Plan for the whole bill

Compute is one line item. Storage, backups, data transfer, software and support belong in the same conversation.

The migration approach

A planned move.
A tested destination.

Migration is an application change, not just a data transfer. Dependencies and recovery plans matter as much as the destination.

Discover and map

Inventory services, dependencies, data volumes and access requirements. Identify workloads that should move together.

Build and rehearse

Validate performance, backup restoration and integrations in the target environment. Document the cutover and rollback criteria.

Move and verify

Check data consistency, application behavior and monitoring after the move. Retire the source only when validation is complete.
